NEW · FLASH WEB

Now in your browser.
Now with your voice.

Run flash --web and the same agent opens in a window as easy as the chat apps you already know. Talk to it, drop files on it, pick it up on your phone. Same model, same tools, same permission prompts. Still on your machine.

Watch it work

Replies stream in as they're written. Tool calls fold into rows you can open, plans tick off as they land, and every file change shows as a colored diff.

Talk to it

With the box empty, the send button turns into a voice button. FLASH listens, answers, and reads the reply aloud, highlighting each sentence as it goes, all on your machine. Say "interrupt", "pause", "resume", or "stop voice", and answer its permission prompts out loud.

Attach anything

Drag files onto the chat, paste a screenshot, or open the + menu to add photos, grab a screenshot, or point the message at a skill.

Steer while it works

Type while FLASH is busy and it reads your message at its next step. Ctrl Enter queues it for after the turn instead.

Projects

A folder plus instructions. Chats sit under their project in the sidebar, run their tools in its folder, and keep their history across restarts.

Everything on a key

Ctrl K searches every action, chat, and model, and Alt F searches inside your chats. Hover any button to see its shortcut.

Files beside the chat

The images, PDFs, and pages FLASH makes slide out in a panel on the right. Pages open sandboxed, so their scripts never touch FLASH.

On your phone

Turn on network access in Settings and scan the QR code. Settings also lists every signed-in browser, so you can sign one out, or all the others with a fresh link.

Settings that matter

Usage stats and streaks, memory you can edit or import from another AI, voice models sized for your machine, backgrounds, light and dark, and one-click updates.
