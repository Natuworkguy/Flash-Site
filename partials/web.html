FLASH WEB

The same agent,
in your browser.

Run flash --web and FLASH opens in a window that feels like the chat apps you already know: the same model, the same tools, the same permission prompts. It still runs on your machine.

Watch it work

Replies stream in as they're written. Tool calls fold into rows you can open, plans tick off as they land, and every file change shows as a colored diff.

Projects

A folder plus instructions. Every chat in a project runs its tools there, and chats keep their history across restarts.

Everything on a key

Ctrl K searches every action, chat, and model, and Alt F searches inside your chats. Hover any button to see its shortcut.

Files beside the chat

The images, PDFs, and pages FLASH makes slide out in a panel on the right. Pages open sandboxed, so their scripts never touch FLASH.

On your phone

Add --lan and scan the QR code to pick it up from your phone. Every request needs the link's random token.

Settings that matter

Usage stats and streaks, memory you can edit or import from another AI, light and dark, and one-click updates.
