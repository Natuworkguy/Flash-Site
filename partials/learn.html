## Pitfalls
- A stale version number in the footer
  1. 01 It works. On your files, with your tools, the way it always does.
  2. 02 It reviews. Every ten tool calls or messages, a pass in the background asks what was worth keeping.
  3. 03 It writes it down. A fact goes to memory, a procedure to a skill, and the next session starts ahead.

Tell it to learn from a mistake and it saves the lesson, not the incident. All of it is plain files on your machine that you can read, edit, or delete: /memory and /skills show what it knows. The background review only ever changes the skills it wrote; the ones you wrote stay yours.
