Most AI tools phone home to a server you will never see. FLASH does not. Point it at localhost, or at any machine on your own network. One environment variable decides where the intelligence lives: your models, your hardware, your data, staying exactly where you put it.

Other AI coding tools can look local too: they run shell commands and read files right on your machine. But the model doing the actual thinking is still sitting on someone else's servers, and everything you send it, your files, your commands, your code, travels there first. FLASH doesn't fake it. The tools run locally. The model runs locally. Your data never leaves the building unless you tell it to. The one way to tell it to is opt-in and clearly labeled: a cloudbase tag, which runs the model on a hosted base and says so before you pull it.

Local OLLAMA_HOST unset http://localhost:11434
Remote OLLAMA_HOST= http://192.168.1.50:11434
